Brahmagupta's Balance — One Equation, Three Unknowns

The equation 1x + 2y + 3z = 0 has one equation but three unknowns. This gives infinitely many solutions — a whole plane of them. Brahmagupta, who gave us the rules for zero, would appreciate that the solution space itself is a 2D plane in 3D space.
