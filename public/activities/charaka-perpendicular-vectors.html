Charaka's Vectors — What is Perpendicular to [1, 1]?

Two vectors are perpendicular when their dot product is zero. For v = [1, 1], this means v · u = 0 ⇒ 1·a + 1·b = 0 ⇒ b = −a. So every vector of the form [t, −t] is perpendicular to [1, 1]. Find them.
