Panini's Grammar of Space — ℝ, ℝ², ℝ³

Panini, the ancient grammarian, classified words into dimensions: a single sound (1D), a root (2D), a complete sentence (3D). In the same way, a point lives in ℝ (one number), ℝ² (two numbers), or ℝ³ (three numbers). Locate points at each level and feel what "dimension" means.

ℝ — 1 number ℝ² — 2 numbers (x, y) ℝ³ — 3 numbers (x, y, z)
