The Right Angle — Row Space ⟂ Nullspace

For A = [[1,2,3],[4,5,6],[7,8,9]], every vector in the row space ℛ(A) is perpendicular to every vector in the nullspace 𝒩(A). This is the Fundamental Theorem of Linear Algebra: the row space and nullspace are orthogonal complements in ℝ³.
