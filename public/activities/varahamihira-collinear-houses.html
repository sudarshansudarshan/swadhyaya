Locking it in

You just found (or saw) a line through all three posts. Let's put words and an equation to what you discovered.

1. Jyoti (2,1), Graha (3,2) and Nakṣatra (4,3) —

2. Going from Jyoti to Graha, x increases by 1 and y increases by 1. Going from Graha to Nakṣatra?

3. A constant rate of change (same rise for the same run, every step) is the signature of…

Since y is always 1 less than x at every post, the equation of the line is:

y = x − 1
Open GeoGebra ↗ — type y = x - 1 to verify

Now generalize it

What if Varāhamihira's first walk wasn't 2 units, or each turn-step wasn't 1 unit? Drag the sliders and watch where the line ends up — the slope never changes, only where it crosses the axes.
