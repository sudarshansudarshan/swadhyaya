Tenali's Number Trick — What is f(5)?

Tenali's number trick: pick a number x, square it, then subtract 10. The result is called f(x). So f(x) = x² − 10. Try the trick five times for x = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 — by the end you'll have computed f(5).

pick x → square (x²) − 10 = f(x) = x² − 10
