Vyasa's Plane — Every (x, y, z) Perpendicular to W

From the previous activity, W = {β(2, 7, 3) | β ∈ ℝ} is a line through the origin with direction (2, 7, 3). For any (w₁, w₂, w₃) in W, find every (x, y, z) with [w₁ w₂ w₃] · [x; y; z] = 0. The answer is not a single point or a line — it's an entire plane through the origin.

w₁ w₂ w₃ · x y z = 0 ⇒ 2x + 7y + 3z = 0
