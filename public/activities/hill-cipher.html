The Hill Cipher — Matrices as Encryption Keys

Every letter becomes a number (A=0, B=1, …, Z=25). Pair them up, multiply by the key matrix A, and the message is encrypted. Decrypt using A⁻¹. This is the Hill Cipher — one of the first cipher systems to use matrix algebra.

Plaintext → numbers A × pair = cipher A⁻¹ × cipher = plaintext
